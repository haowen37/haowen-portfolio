NEAR HORIZON

VIEW PROJECT

Some places only feel far. Near Horizon is an electric mobility concept for the places beyond the usual route. A lake outside the city. A friend across town. Somewhere the last stop doesn’t quite reach. Through a precise visual identity, considered product design and an open digital experience, the brand brings one idea to life: make far feel close.
