Our point of view

Good to know.

What’s the difference between One and Step?

One has a diamond frame. Step has a low step-through frame for easier everyday access. Both share the same Near Horizon approach to electric mobility.

Can I remove the battery?

Yes. The battery is integrated into the frame and can be removed.

Where are they designed to ride?

City streets and light gravel. Both have semi-wide tyres and a comfortable urban / all-road riding position.
